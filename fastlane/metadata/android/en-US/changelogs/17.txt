AgePony 5.0.1 (includes 5.0.0)

New:
- Share sheet and text-selection support
- Hardware decryption keys (age1tag)
- Decrypt with a YubiKey over NFC
- Move keys to another phone or onto paper
- Passphrase tools without unlocking
- Signed files open with plain age

Security:
- A duress PIN makes the vault PIN-only
- Vault locks properly after sharing
- Waits after wrong PINs
- Screenshots blocked by default
- Transfer code on both phones
- Stronger signatures, stricter file checks
